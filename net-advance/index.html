A lower rate.

02 / READ THE RESULT

A cash test,
not a ranking of loans.

In the starting example, both structures offer £3.3 million against a £3 million redemption. Each deducts a 2% arrangement fee and £20,000 of other costs.

Structure A: 0.65% a month retained for 12 months uses £257,400. Net proceeds are £2,956,600, leaving a £43,400 shortfall.

Structure B: 0.85% a month retained for six months uses £168,300. Net proceeds are £3,045,700, leaving £45,700 after redemption.

The difference comes from the retention period. Hold all other inputs equal and a lower rate improves the net advance. Six months retained does not establish a six-month loan term or prove the interest reserve will last until repayment. Any later interest still needs funding.

A positive balance does not establish eligibility, adequate security or a viable repayment route. A shortfall identifies the cash that this simplified completion statement does not cover. Discuss the full structure alongside the development exit refinance requirements.

03 / METHOD & LIMITS

Every deduction, visible.

Retained interest = gross facility × monthly rate ÷ 100 × reserve months
Net advance = gross facility − retained interest − arrangement fee − other costs
Completion balance = net advance − existing loan redemption

Interest is simple, calculated on the full gross facility for the selected period. The arrangement fee is a percentage of the gross facility and is deducted once. No fee is added to the loan. Results are calculated without intermediate rounding and displayed to the nearest pound; CSV values include pence.

The indicative gross needed to cover redemption solves: (redemption + other costs) ÷ (1 − monthly rate × reserve months ÷ 100 − fee ÷ 100). If the denominator is zero or negative, no finite positive facility solves this model. This calculation is not a loan approval or a valuation test.

The tool excludes compounding, staged drawdowns, interest on a changing balance, reserve rebates, servicing interest from sales, early repayments, exit charges on the new loan, extension charges and taxes unless you include them in the appropriate input. It does not model net sales proceeds or maturity repayment. Actual facility documents may define the rate base, retention and fees differently.
